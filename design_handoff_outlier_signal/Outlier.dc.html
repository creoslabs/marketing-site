#gymtips

#formcheck
#liftheavy
Description
Single take to camera, phone at chest height in a commercial gym, no intro card. He holds eye contact for four seconds, then cuts to three short inserts of the mistake, each stamped with large on-screen numbering. Back to camera for the one-sentence fix. Burned-in captions throughout, no music under the voice, hard cut to black on the CTA.

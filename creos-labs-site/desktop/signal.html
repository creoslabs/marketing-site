FAQ

Questions.

Which formats can Signal score?−

Static images and video ads — each format is scored against its own criteria, so a video score and a static score are never averaged together.

What does the score compare against?+

Every other asset you’ve analysed in that format — so an 82 tells you where this ad sits in your own work, not an abstract average.

Can I review a whole campaign at once?+

Yes. Upload a round of creative together and every score lands side by side.
